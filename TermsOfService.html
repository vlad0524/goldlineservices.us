We value your privacy. The phone numbers obtained through the SMS consent process will not be shared with third parties or affiliate companies for marketing purposes.

2. Types of SMS Communications:
By opting in to receive text messages from our company, you may receive messages related to the following:

○ Order and inquiry updates

○ Promotional offers (if applicable and consented to)
Example: “Hello, we've received your inquiry. Let us review and get back to you. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messaging at any time.”

Message frequency may vary based on your interactions with our services. For example, you may receive up to a few messages per week regarding order updates and service notifications.

4. Potential Fees for SMS Messaging:
Standard message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier’s pricing plan.

5. Opt-In Method:
You may opt-in to receive SMS messages from us by checking the SMS consent box on our website or by opting in via customer service representatives.

6. Opt-Out Method:
You can opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying “STOP” to any message you receive, or by contacting our support team directly to request removal from our messaging list.

7. Help:
If you experience any issues, reply with the keyword “HELP” for assistance, or contact us directly.

We reserve the right to update or modify these SMS Terms & Conditions at any time. Changes will be reflected directly on our website.
